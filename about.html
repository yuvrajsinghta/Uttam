Our Story

How India
Treks

Founded in 2006 with one trail and a bold belief — that the Himalayas should be accessible, safe and life-changing for every Indian.

"The mountains don't care about your job title. They only care about how honest you are with yourself."

Where It All Began

Shikhar started as a single Kedarkantha batch of 12 trekkers led by our founder Sandhya Kumar — a biologist with a deep love for alpine ecosystems and an equally deep frustration at how poorly the mountains were being treated by early adventure tourism.

That first trek changed 12 lives. Word spread. By 2010, we had 200 trekkers. By 2015, we had documented 100 trails and built India's first dedicated trek safety training programme. Today, we've taken 35,000+ trekkers across 500+ documented trails — and removed more waste from the mountains than we have ever brought in.

We are still led by the same belief as Day 1: the mountains deserve our respect, and trekkers deserve honest, safe, transformative experiences.

Our Journey

Milestones That
Shaped Us

2006

First Trek — Kedarkantha

12 trekkers, one guide, a rented tent. The journey that started everything.

2009

Green Trails Initiative Launched

We became the first trekking organisation in India to adopt a mandatory zero-waste trek policy for all batches.

2013

100 Trails Documented

Our GPS documentation project created India's most accurate trekking database — available to all, for free.

2016

Wilderness First Responder Certification Mandatory

Every Shikhar trek leader must be WFR certified — still a rare standard in Indian adventure tourism.

2020

10,000 Trekker Milestone

Zero serious incidents across all treks. We recommit to the same safety standards that got us here.

2025

35,000+ Trekkers, 500+ Trails

2,000+ kg of waste removed. Still growing. Still learning. Still humbled by the mountains every single day.

What Drives Us

Our Values

01

Leave No Trace

Not a slogan. A non-negotiable rule on every single Shikhar trek. Every trekker carries out more than they carry in.

02

Safety Above Summits

We turn back when the mountain says so. No peak is worth a life. Our guides are empowered — always — to make that call.

03

Local Communities First

100% of our porters and cooks are hired from mountain villages. We reinvest directly into the communities that let us use their trails.

04

Radical Honesty

We tell you exactly what a trek demands. We don't oversell. We don't create false expectations. We respect you too much for that.

05

Documentation for All

Our 500+ trail maps are freely available to anyone — trekking companies included. The mountains belong to everyone.

06

Transformation Over Tourism

We measure success by how different our trekkers feel on Day 6 versus Day 1. That's the only metric that matters to us.
