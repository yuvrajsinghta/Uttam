Where It All Began

From One Trail to
India's Most Trusted

Shikhar started as a single Kedarkantha batch of 12 trekkers led by our founder Sandhya Kumar — a biologist with a deep love for alpine ecosystems and an equally deep frustration at how poorly the mountains were being treated by early adventure tourism.

That first trek changed 12 lives. Word spread. By 2010, we had 200 trekkers. By 2015, we had documented 100 trails and built India's first dedicated trek safety training programme. Today, 35,000+ trekkers have trusted us across 500+ documented trails.

We are still led by the same belief as Day 1: the mountains deserve our respect, and trekkers deserve honest, safe, transformative experiences — not Instagram-optimised shortcuts.

Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Us
2006

First Trek — Kedarkantha, 12 Trekkers

One rented tent, one passionate guide, twelve curious people. The journey that started everything Shikhar is today.

2009

Green Trails Initiative Launched

We became the first trekking organisation in India to adopt mandatory zero-waste protocols for every single batch — before it was fashionable.

2012

Wilderness First Responder Standard Set

All Shikhar trek leaders required to hold WFR certification — a 70-hour medical training programme. Still rare in Indian adventure tourism.

2015

100 Trails Documented — Free for Everyone

Our GPS documentation project created India's most accurate trekking database, available free to all — trekkers, guides and competing companies alike.

2019

Satellite Communication on All Treks

Garmin inReach satellite communicators deployed across all batches. Real-time tracking and SOS at any altitude, any signal condition.

2023

India's Best Trek Organisation Award

Adventure Tourism Awards 2023. Accepted with gratitude — and with the reminder that we earn it fresh on every single trek.

2025

35,000+ Trekkers · 500+ Trails · Zero Incidents

Still growing. Still learning. Still humbled by the mountains every single day. The mission remains exactly what it was in 2006.

What Drives Us

Our Values
01

Leave No Trace

Not a slogan. A non-negotiable. Every trekker carries out more than they carry in. We've removed 2,000+ kg of waste from Indian trails and counting.

02

Safety Above Summits

We turn back when the mountain says so. No peak is worth a life. Our guides have unconditional authority to abort any trek, any time, for any safety reason.

03

Local Communities First

100% of our porters, cooks and base staff are hired from mountain villages. We reinvest directly into the communities whose trails we use.

04

Radical Honesty

We tell you exactly what a trek demands. No overselling. No false expectations. We respect you too much to market a fantasy instead of the real thing.

05

Documentation for All

Our 500+ trail maps are freely available to anyone — trekkers, other companies, researchers. The mountains belong to everyone, not to any company.

06

Transformation Over Tourism

We measure success by how different our trekkers feel on Day 6 vs Day 1. That inner shift — not the summit photo — is the only metric that matters to us.
