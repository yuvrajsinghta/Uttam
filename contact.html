Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What fitness level is needed for Kedarkantha?

Moderate fitness — able to walk 5–7 km uphill for 3–4 hours a day with a light daypack. No prior trekking experience required, but being generally active helps enormously.

What exactly is included in the price?

All camping gear, trek leader, cook, support staff, all meals from Day 1 dinner, forest permits, first aid with oxygen cylinder, guesthouse on Day 1 and Garmin satellite communicator.

How do I get to the trek start point?

Most treks start from Dehradun, Rishikesh or Haridwar. We share detailed transport instructions 2 weeks before. We can also arrange shared transport — just ask when booking.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 15 days before. 50% refund 8–14 days before. No refund within 7 days — but we'll happily reschedule you to a future batch at no extra charge.

Is it safe for solo women trekkers?

Absolutely. 40% of our trekkers are solo women. Groups are carefully curated, our guides are trained in inclusivity, and we offer women-only batches for select treks on request.

Do you offer corporate or school group treks?

Yes — we design fully custom corporate retreats and school programmes with team-building modules, safety briefings and flexible scheduling. Contact us for a tailored quote.

What if I get altitude sickness?

Every Shikhar trek leader is trained in AMS, HACE and HAPE recognition and treatment. We carry portable oxygen and pulse oximeters. If symptoms are serious, descent begins immediately — no discussion.

Can I bring children on a trek?

Yes, for Easy-rated treks, children above 10 years can join with parent/guardian approval and a medical fitness form. We recommend Kedarkantha or Deoriatal for families with older children.
