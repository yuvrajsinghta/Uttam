Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What fitness level do I need for Kedarkantha?

Moderate fitness — able to walk 5–7 km a day with a light daypack. No prior trekking experience needed, but being comfortable walking uphill for 3–4 hours is important.

What's included in the trek cost?

All camping equipment, trek leader, cook & support staff, all meals from Day 1 dinner onwards, permits, first aid with oxygen cylinder, and any Shikhar base camp stays.

How do I get to the trek base?

Most treks start from Dehradun, Rishikesh or Haridwar. We share detailed transport info 2 weeks before your trek. We can help arrange shared vehicles too.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 15 days before the trek. 50% refund 8–15 days before. No refund within 7 days — but we'll help you reschedule to a future batch at no cost.

Can solo women trek safely with Shikhar?

Absolutely. 40% of our trekkers are solo women. Our groups are curated, guides are trained in inclusivity, and we have women-only batches for select treks on request.

Do you offer corporate or school group treks?

Yes — we design custom corporate and school programmes with team-building elements, safety briefings and flexible scheduling. Contact us for a custom quote.
