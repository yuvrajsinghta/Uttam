About This Trek

Kedarkantha is one of the most popular winter treks in India, and for good reason. Standing at 3,800 meters, the summit offers panoramic views of Swargarohini, Kalanag, and the majestic Bandarpoonch peaks. The trail winds through dense pine forests, open meadows dusted with snow, and ancient shepherd villages.

What's Included

All camping equipment, professional trek leader, cook and support staff, all meals from Day 1 dinner to Day 6 breakfast, forest permits, first aid kit with oxygen cylinder, and comprehensive trek documentation.

We Changed How India Treks

Founded in 2006 with a single trail and a bold dream — to make the Himalayas accessible, safe, and transformative for every Indian.

Our Mission
More Than Adventure
We believe trekking has the power to transform lives. Every trek we design is built around that belief — connecting people with nature, with each other, and with themselves.

🌿 Environmental Responsibility

Our Green Trails initiative has cleaned over 2,000 kg of waste from mountain trails. We leave every campsite cleaner than we found it — always.

🛡️ Safety Above All

Every trek leader is Wilderness First Responder certified. We carry satellite phones, oxygen cylinders, and maintain real-time emergency protocols.

🗺️ Documentation First

We documented Kedarkantha, Rupin Pass, and Buran Ghati when they were just shepherd paths. We now have India's largest trekking documentation library.

🤝 Community Built

We work with and empower local mountain communities — providing livelihoods, training, and sustainable income through responsible tourism.

💡 Education Through Experience

Every trek is a classroom. Trekkers learn navigation, survival skills, ecology, and mindfulness as part of the journey — not as an afterthought.

♾️ Transformation Focus

The person who starts a trek and the person who returns are rarely the same. We design every touchpoint to facilitate genuine inner growth.
