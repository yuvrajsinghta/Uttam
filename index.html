Safety First, Always

Your Safety Is Non-Negotiable

18 years. 35,000+ trekkers. Zero serious incidents. Here's how we maintain that record.

🏥

Wilderness First Responder

Every Shikhar trek leader carries WFR certification — a 70-hour medical training programme designed for remote emergencies. We don't do basic first aid.

WFR Certified
📡

Satellite Communication

Garmin inReach satellite communicators on every trek. Real-time GPS tracking, two-way messaging and SOS capability — even at 5,000m with no cell signal.

Garmin inReach
🫁

Altitude Medicine Protocol

Portable oxygen, pulse oximeters and Diamox available on all high-altitude treks. Our leaders are trained to recognise and treat AMS, HACE and HAPE.

AMS Protocol
🌦️

Weather Intelligence

We subscribe to IMD and mountain-specific forecasting services. Route changes are made 48 hours in advance when conditions are marginal — never on the trail.

Real-time Data
🏔️

Route Abort Protocols

Our guides have unconditional authority to abort any trek at any point for safety reasons. No peer pressure, no client pressure overrides this. Ever.

Guide Authority
📋

Pre-trek Medical Screening

Every trekker completes a health questionnaire reviewed by our medical team. We match fitness levels to trails — and honestly tell you if a trek isn't right for you yet.

Health Verified
