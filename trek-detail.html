About This Trek

Kedarkantha is one of the most celebrated winter treks in India, and it earns that reputation every single season. Standing at 3,800 metres, the summit offers panoramic views of Swargarohini, Kalanag, Bandarpoonch and the Khatling range — a Himalayan canvas that stops even seasoned trekkers in their tracks.

The trail winds through dense pine forests dusted with snow, open meadows, and ancient shepherd villages that feel frozen in another century. In winter, the entire landscape becomes pristine white — making it one of the most photographed treks in the country. Even on cloudy days, the journey itself is deeply rewarding.

This trek is genuinely accessible to beginners — but it is not without challenge. A 3 AM summit push through knee-deep snow, navigating steep ridgelines in low light, and the cold that bites at altitude are all very real. What Shikhar provides is the preparation, protocols and people to make those challenges feel manageable and ultimately triumphant.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

1
Dehradun → Sankri (Drive + Arrival)

Shared vehicle from Dehradun ISBT at 6 AM. Arrive Sankri by afternoon. Rest, acclimatise, meet your trek leader. Gear check and briefing. Overnight at guesthouse.

📍 Sankri: 1,920m🚗 Drive: ~9 hrs
2
Sankri → Juda Ka Talab (Trek begins)

Trek through oak and rhododendron forests to the frozen lake of Juda Ka Talab. The forest is dense, atmospheric and absolutely magical in winter snow.

📍 2,750m📏 4.5 km⏱ 4–5 hrs
3
Juda Ka Talab → Kedarkantha Base Camp

Short but steep climb to a wide alpine meadow at the base of the summit. Stunning views open as the treeline thins. Rest and sleep early — summit day tomorrow.

📍 3,450m📏 4 km⏱ 3–4 hrs
4
SUMMIT DAY — Kedarkantha Peak 🏔

Pre-dawn start at 3 AM. A demanding climb through deep snow leads to the 3,800m summit by sunrise. The 360° panoramic views are among the most spectacular in all of India. Descend back to Sankri via a different trail route.

📍 3,800m SUMMIT📏 13 km⏱ 8–10 hrs
5
Buffer Day at Sankri

Scheduled rest and weather buffer day. If conditions were perfect, use this for local village exploration and celebration. If weather delayed Day 4 — this day saves the summit.

📍 Sankri: 1,920m
6
Sankri → Dehradun (Drive home)

Morning departure from Sankri. Arrive Dehradun by evening. Trek ends here. Carry these memories — and this version of yourself — for the rest of your life.

🚗 Drive: ~9 hrs
