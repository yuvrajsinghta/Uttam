About This Trek

Kedarkantha is one of the most popular winter treks in India, and for good reason. Standing at 3,800 metres, the summit offers panoramic views of Swargarohini, Kalanag, Bandarpoonch and the majestic Khatling range.

The trail winds through dense pine forests laden with snow, open meadows, and centuries-old shepherd villages. In winter, the entire landscape transforms into a pristine white canvas — making it one of the most photogenic treks in the country.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

1
Dehradun → Sankri (Overnight Drive)

Board a shared vehicle from Dehradun. Arrive at Sankri — our base village at 1,920m. Rest and acclimatise. Briefing from your trek leader.

📍 Altitude: 1,920m
2
Sankri → Juda Ka Talab

Trek through oak and rhododendron forests to the frozen lake of Juda Ka Talab. Campsite here is magical in winter.

📍 2,750m📏 4.5 km⏱ 4–5 hrs
3
Juda Ka Talab → Kedarkantha Base Camp

Short but steep climb to a wide alpine meadow at the base of the summit. Stunning views emerge as the treeline thins.

📍 3,450m📏 4 km⏱ 3–4 hrs
4
Summit Day — Kedarkantha Peak

Pre-dawn start (3 AM). A steep climb through deep snow leads to the 3,800m summit by sunrise. 360° panoramic views. Return to Sankri via a different trail.

📍 3,800m SUMMIT📏 13 km⏱ 8–9 hrs
5
Buffer / Rest Day at Sankri

Scheduled rest day for weather contingency or to rest sore muscles. Optional village exploration.

6
Sankri → Dehradun (Drive)

Morning drive back. Arrive Dehradun by afternoon. Trek ends here — carry memories for a lifetime.
